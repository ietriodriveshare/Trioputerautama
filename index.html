Our Products

Provides leather tannery for gloves, shoes, bags and garments using sheep skin, goat skin and cow hide. Headquarter in Jakarta, with Factory at Bogor, Indonesia. We primarily produce high-quality natural leather used in Leather Gloves (Golf Gloves, Batting Gloves, Working Gloves, Motorcycle Gloves, Horse Riding Gloves). Leather for Shoes like nappa (Nappa ni, Softy Nappa, Drymill, Lining). Leather for Bags (Fix Pelleteria, Nappa Porto, Nappa Corrected). Leather For Garments (Avanza, Solerno, Armani, Fashion). PRODUCTION PROCESS

Our production process combines skilled craftsmanship and strict quality control to deliver best quality leather product consistently.

STEP 01

MATERIAL
SELECTION

We carefully select best-quality raw materials to ensure the best results.

STEP 02

TANNING
RETANNING

Raw material go through a precise tanning and retanning process to achieve optimal quality.

STEP 03

DRYING
MILLING

Ensuring the leather is shaped to the desired softness.

STEP 04

STACKING
TOGGLE

We’re ensuring the skin surface is fully stretched without wrinkles.

STEP 05

QUALITY
CONTROL

Every leather undergoes strict quality control to meet our high standards.

STEP 06

PACKAGING
DELIVERY

Finished products are carefully packaged and prepared for timely delivery to our customers worldwide.

STEP 07

PROPERTY TEST (Chemical & Physical Test)

Comprehensive chemical & physical laboratory testing for tensile strength, tear resistance, colorfastness, and safety compliance.
